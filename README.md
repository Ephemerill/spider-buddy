# Spider Buddy

A friendly jumping spider that lives on your Mac's screen. It crawls the edges
of your display, leaps between your open windows, walks along the Dock, rappels
from the menu bar on a silk thread, and can be picked up and thrown around.

## Install

1. Grab the `.dmg` from the [latest release](https://github.com/Ephemerill/spider-buddy/releases/latest).
2. Open it and drag Spider to Applications.

Needs macOS 13 or later. The app is signed and notarized, and updates itself.

## What it does

**Walks anywhere there's an edge.** The border of your screen, around every
open window (on top, down the sides, hanging underneath), along the Dock, and
under the menu bar. It rides along when you move or resize a window.

**Moves like a real jumping spider.** Short bursts of walking, a look around,
a crouch and wiggle before a leap, and a squash on landing. It turns, waves,
drums, dances, rolls, grooms, naps and wanders off on its own.

**Plays with you.**
- **Drag and throw it.** Flick it and it flies; throw it hard enough and it
  catches itself on a web.
- **Click, double-click, stroke or scroll it** for waves, wiggles, hearts, or
  to send it up or down a thread.
- **Move the pointer near it** and it notices, turns to look, and follows
  along. Fidget the pointer like something alive and it will pounce.
- **Toys:** a laser pointer, ball, feather, bell or wind-up bug.
- **Feed it** a cricket, worm, fly, moth, beetle, ant, mosquito or ladybug.

**Spins silk.** It swings from window corners, rappels down from ledges,
climbs back up hand over hand, and builds hammocks to nap in. Strands of silk
are left around your desktop and fade away.

**Hides from windows.** When a window comes down over it, it ducks out of the
way. It can also play peek-a-boo behind a window's edge.

**Has manners.** It lies down in a corner when a film goes full screen, can
be kept in a box you draw on the desktop, and gets sleepy in Low Power Mode.

**Reacts to your Mac.** Jumps at notifications, perks up when you plug in the
charger, and notices rain where you are.

**Climbs web pages.** With a browser filling the screen, it climbs along the
tops of cards, bars and boxes on the page. This is off by default for now.

## Make it yours: Spider Studio

Open **Spider Studio…** (⌘,) from the menu. Every change shows up on your
desktop immediately.

- **Looks:** 11 body shapes, 13 eye styles, 38 hats, 30 extras to wear, 13 leg
  styles, 60 coats (including rainbow, lava and camouflage), and 20 markings.
- **Personality:** pick a temperament (Friendly, Shy, Hyper, Lazy, Curious,
  Show-off, Chill, Grumpy) or set six sliders.
- **Gait and habits:** how it walks, and a dial for each thing it does on its
  own.
- **Thoughts:** chit-chat, encouragement, spider facts or Bible verses, plus
  your own lines.
- **A name:** rest the pointer on it and a name tag appears.

It also learns: stroke it daily and a shy spider slowly gets bolder.

## The habitat

**Open Habitat** gives it a glass terrarium to live in, with a painted
scene, weather, day and night, furniture to climb, and a build-it-yourself
house kit. It finds its own favourite spots, drinks, hunts, shelters from the
rain and sleeps. Save your tanks and come back to them later.

## Using it

Right-click the spider (or use the menu bar icon) for the menu:

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Come Here | it walks or jumps to your pointer |
| Behavior ▸ | Say Hi, Toss It, Swing!, Feed, hammock, keep it in a box |
| Size, Energy | Tiny to Chonky, Sleepy to Caffeinated |
| Follow / Pounce on the Cursor | whether it cares about, or hunts, your pointer |
| Shoot Webs, Build Hammocks | turn its silk habits on or off |
| Click to Pick Up | turn off to make it fully click-through |
| Pause, Hide | freeze it, or put it away |
| Launch at Login | start with your Mac |
| Check for Updates | looks for a new version |
| Bring it to the Middle | if you lose it |

Clicks pass straight through to whatever is underneath unless the pointer is
actually on the spider.

## Permissions

Nothing is required. If you allow **Screen Recording**, it can match its
camouflage to what is behind it, measure window corners more accurately, and
climb web pages. Pictures are looked at and never kept, and nothing is ever
sent anywhere.
